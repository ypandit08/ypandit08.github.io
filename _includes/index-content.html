PhD Student · Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai

Hi 👋, I am a PhD student in the School of Technology and Computer Science at TIFR, Mumbai, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Umang Bhaskar. Before joining TIFR, I worked at Intel Corporation in Bangalore. I hold an M.Tech. from NIT Karnataka and a B.E. from the University of Mumbai. After completing my B.E., I worked briefly at Tata Consultancy Services.

My research interests lie in theoretical computer science, particularly in algorithms, graph theory, algorithmic game theory, and topics with a combinatorial flavor. I am currently working on problems in fair division. Specifically, my work focuses on understanding when fair allocations exist and whether they can be computed efficiently.
